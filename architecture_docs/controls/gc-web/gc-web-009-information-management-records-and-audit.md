# GC-WEB-009: Information Management, Records, And Audit

Type: Control
Status: Active
Source: Government of Canada

## Intent

Identify business information and data and manage it through its lifecycle.

## Required Outcome

The application identifies information and data of business value and manages it
through its lifecycle.

## Assessment

Confirm the application:

- identifies records, transactional data, audit data, generated documents,
  uploads, exports, and decision records
- records metadata, ownership, stewardship, access, retention, disposition, and
  archival expectations where applicable
- supports import, export, search, retrieval, and interoperability where needed
- logs audit events for sensitive, privileged, or business-critical actions
- avoids deleting or overwriting records without a defined retention and
  disposition path

## Evidence Examples

- information management review note
- audit event shape
- retention and disposition rule
- export or records retrieval check
- metadata decision
